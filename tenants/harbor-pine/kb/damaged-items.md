# Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or stops working because of a defect within 1 year of delivery, we make it right at no cost:

1. Contact us within 14 days of delivery for shipping damage (1 year for manufacturing defects) with your order number and a photo of the damage.
2. We send a free replacement, or a full refund if the item is out of stock.
3. You don't need to return damaged items unless we ask.

This applies to all items, including final-sale items.
