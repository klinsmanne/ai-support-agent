# Returns Policy

## Standard returns
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused, unwashed and in original packaging. Return shipping is free for customers in the US using our prepaid label; customers in Canada and Mexico pay return shipping.

## Sale and clearance items
Items marked "final sale" (including clearance candles and seasonal decor) cannot be returned or exchanged. The only exception is an item that arrives damaged or defective — those are always covered (see Damaged or Defective Items).

## Bedding and textiles
Duvets, sheets and pillows can be returned within 30 days only if the original packaging is unopened, for hygiene reasons.

## How to start a return
Reply to your order confirmation email or contact support with your order number. We email a prepaid return label (US) within one business day. Refunds are issued within 2 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.

## Exchanges
We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order.
