# Refunds and Billing

## Refund timing
Once a refund is issued, it appears on your statement within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

## Duplicate charges
If you see two identical charges for one order, one is usually a temporary authorization that disappears within 3 business days. If both charges are captured (marked "posted"), contact us with the order number and we will refund the duplicate immediately.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order, we refund the difference once per order. Clearance prices are excluded.

## Payment methods
We accept major credit cards, PayPal and Harbor & Pine gift cards. We never ask for your full card number by email or chat.
