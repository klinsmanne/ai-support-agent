# Shipping Policy

Harbor & Pine ships to the United States, Canada and Mexico.

## Processing time
Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1–2 business days. Orders placed after 2 pm Pacific on Friday ship the following Monday.

## Delivery times
- Standard shipping (US): 3–7 business days after dispatch. Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95.
- Express shipping (US): 1–3 business days after dispatch, $19.95.
- Canada and Mexico: 6–12 business days after dispatch. Duties may apply at delivery.
- Large items such as rugs ship by freight and take 5–10 business days.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email when your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed shipments
If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or your order is more than 5 business days past the estimated delivery date, contact us. We open a trace with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund — your choice.
