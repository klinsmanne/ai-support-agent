# Account Access and Security

## Password resets
Support staff and our AI assistant can never reset passwords or change login emails over email or chat. Use the self-service reset at https://cadence.example/reset — a link is emailed to the address on the account.

## Locked out of SSO
If your company uses SSO and you are locked out, contact your workspace administrator. Only a workspace owner can change SSO settings.

## Two-factor authentication
If you lost your 2FA device, use one of your backup codes. Without backup codes, the workspace owner must open an identity-verified request; this is always handled by a human.
