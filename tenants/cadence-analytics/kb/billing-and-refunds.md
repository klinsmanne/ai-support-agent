# Billing and Refunds

## Monthly plans
Monthly plans renew automatically. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period. Monthly payments are non-refundable, except for billing errors such as duplicate charges, which we refund in full.

## Annual plans
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, we offer a prorated refund for unused full months only when you downgrade to a lower annual tier; all such requests are reviewed by our billing team.

## Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, contact support with the invoice number (for example INV-2042). We verify the charge and refund the duplicate within 1 business day.

## Invoices
Invoices are available under Settings → Billing. We can re-issue an invoice with a different company name or VAT number on request.
