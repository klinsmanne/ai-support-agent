# Plans and Seats

- **Starter** — $19/month, 1 seat, 3 dashboards.
- **Pro** — $49/month, 3 seats, unlimited dashboards, scheduled reports.
- **Team** — $24 per seat/month billed annually, minimum 10 seats, SSO and audit logs.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Seats can be added at any time from Settings → Members.
