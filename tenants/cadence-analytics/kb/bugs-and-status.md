# Reporting Bugs and Service Status

Check https://status.cadence.example for incidents first. To report a bug, include the dashboard URL, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot. Bug reports are routed to our engineering team, which replies within 2 business days. Severity 1 issues (data loss or full outage) are handled 24/7.

Our uptime target is 99.9% per month for Team plans, with service credits defined in the Team plan agreement.
